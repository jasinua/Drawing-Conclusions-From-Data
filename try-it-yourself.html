  1. About how many sixes would we expect from a fair die in 100 rolls? (two decimals)
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer

  1. State the null hypothesis.
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer
    Indy's true average hitting percentage is 30% (μ = 30).
  2. State the alternative hypothesis.
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer
    Her true average hitting percentage is greater than 30% (μ > 30).
  3. A few of her sets were below 30%. Does that mean she has failed to prove her case?
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer
    No Single sets vary a lot (the standard deviation is about 22.5 points, and 17 of her 50 sets are below 30). What matters is whether the average over all 50 sets is high enough, relative to that variability, to rule out luck.
  4. What is Indy's sample average hitting percentage? (two decimals)

    38.26
  5. What is the standard deviation of her hitting percentage? (two decimals)
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer
    22.52
  6. What is the t-statistic?
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer
    2.59
    With 49 degrees of freedom.t = (38.26 − 30) / (22.52 / √50) = 2.59
  7. What is the p-value?
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer
    0.006
    (one-sided, for μ > 30). The two-sided value is 0.012.
  8. Do we reject or fail to reject the null hypothesis? Why?
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer

  1. What is the null hypothesis for Matthew's A/B test?
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer

  2. What is the alternative hypothesis?
    Show answerHide answer
    Answer
